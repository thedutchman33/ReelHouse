Tyre compound icons used by the Formula 1 analytics views.

These are the standard Pirelli-style compound idents — a black casing, the
compound-coloured ring and the white S / M / H (I / W for the wet tyres)
letter — saved as SVG so the UI never hotlinks a third party at runtime:

  soft.svg          red ring,     "S"
  medium.svg        yellow ring,  "M"
  hard.svg          white ring,   "H"
  intermediate.svg  green ring,   "I"
  wet.svg           blue ring,    "W"

The drawing style follows the icons published by formula-timing.com; they are
generic compound markers rather than team or series trademarks. Replace them
if you ship the app commercially and want artwork of your own.
